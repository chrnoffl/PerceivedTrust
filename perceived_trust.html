THE CASE, WITHOUT THE ANSWER

Some objects are made to help. Someone has taught them to harm.

When a forced confession seems to close three murders, Detective Peter notices the staged objects left behind. Each clue pulls the case further from the obvious answer—and closer to someone inside the investigation.

CASE FILE 01

The principle
inside the crime.

Attractiveness makes us look. Appropriateness asks whether something actually works for the person using it.

Perceived Trust uses that tension as its murder method. A beautiful object may still fail its user. A plain object may do exactly what it must. The film asks us to stop judging by appearance—whether we are looking at a chair, a clue, or a person.
